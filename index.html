Is sustainability guidance actually relevant to Business Process Management?

We collected 425 sustainability guidelines from cloud providers, the Green Software Foundation, W3C, and academic BPM/lean literature. We'd like your judgment on how relevant each one is to designing, modeling, executing, and monitoring business processes — as opposed to being general cloud/software advice. Your ratings, together with an existing AI-generated pass, will help distill a focused Green BPM Guideline out of all this raw guidance.

  • 📝Rate 25 randomly selected guidelines — about 10–15 minutes.
  • 📊For each one: how relevant it is (0–3), which part of the process it touches, and which BPM lifecycle phase it applies to.
  • 🤖Or skip straight to the analysis — see how an AI-generated first pass (Claude) rated all 425 guidelines already.
  • 🔒Anonymous — no account, no personal data collected.
